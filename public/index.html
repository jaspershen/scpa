About Us / 斯坦福中国博士后协会 (SCPA)

The Stanford Chinese Postdoc Association (SCPA) is a vibrant community of Chinese postdocs across all disciplines at Stanford University. Our mission is to promote communication and learning among Chinese postdocs, providing support for their studies, work, and life. We welcome all Chinese postdocs at Stanford to join us!
